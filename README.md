# 三溪寻影 Threebrook Seek

在你自己电脑上运行的 AI 媒体搜索：照你记得的样子说一句话，找到那张照片、视频里的那几秒、那段录音、那份文档。全部在你自己的电脑上处理，什么都不上传。

AI search that runs on your own PC for your photos, videos, recordings and documents. Describe what you remember in one sentence and jump straight to the photo, the right seconds of a video, the recording or the document. Everything runs on your PC; nothing is uploaded.
